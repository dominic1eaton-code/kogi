UNIQUE VALUE PROPOSITION — v3.2
UME OrgOS is the only platform that turns your entire organisation into programmable software — so you can deploy, clone, govern, and optimise it like a tech product.
For scale-up operators and enterprise leaders who are done managing people, processes, and systems in silos — UME replaces spreadsheets, point tools, and tribal knowledge with a live digital twin of your organisation, governed by contracts, connected by data.

⚡ Core USPs — v3.2
Five unique selling propositions · Last updated Dec 18, 2025
USP 01 · OrgOS Factory
The only platform that treats your organisation as software
UME is not a project management tool or a CRM — it is an OrgOS Factory. It produces, manages, and connects entire Organisation Operating Systems. No competitor offers a meta-OS that can provision, clone, and version a whole organisation in <90 seconds.
USP 02 · ODD Actor Governance
Unified contracts for every actor — human, AI, or IoT device
OrgDeviceDrivers (ODDs) apply the same formal governance contract to all actors in your organisation. It does not matter if the actor is a CEO, an AI Agent, or a warehouse sensor — they all operate under the same enforceable contract framework with full audit trails.
USP 03 · Software-Defined Organisation (SDO)
A live digital twin of your entire organisation
The SDO is a real-time, queryable digital twin of your organisation — all running OrgApps, all OrgExecs, all OrgCPUs, and all data flows in one view. Simulate restructures. Detect anomalies. Run optimisation scenarios before touching the real organisation.
USP 04 · OrgDNA — Exportable, Cloneable Config
Deploy new instances of your organisation in 90 seconds
OrgDNA is your org's master config — versioned, exportable, and cloneable. Franchise operators can deploy a fully configured subsidiary from a parent OrgDNA template in under 90 seconds. No other platform offers configuration portability at this level.
USP 05 · DAO & DLT — Multi-Org Governance
Connect multiple organisations under smart contract governance
UME's DAO module connects independent OrgOS instances under a shared distributed ledger and smart contract governance layer. Franchise networks, holding groups, and B2B partnerships can operate with provable, automatic enforcement of inter-org agreements.
